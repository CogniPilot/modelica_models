ACC 2027 preintegration and the current ESKF
7 October 2026

The working-tree ESKF uses the ACC manuscript's retained FOH preintegration
step and consistent physical bias Jacobians. This statement covers the mean
preintegral and its first-order bias correction, not a complete certified
Kalman-filter implementation or a demonstrated universal performance win.

The primary manuscript is ACC_2027_FOH_Preintegration (1).pdf, SHA-256

The older ACC_2027_FOH_Preintegration.pdf has SHA-256

Implementation correspondence

1. preintegrateImuStep applies equation (10)'s trapezoidal body inputs plus
   the signed coning/sculling/scrolling bracket terms, then evaluates the
   mixed SE_2(3) exponential. This is the retained Magnus approximation;
   it is not an exact solution for an arbitrary changing IMU input.
2. Equation (11)'s exponent sensitivities pass through the derivative of
   mixed_increment_matrix. The composed physical blocks are rotation/gyro,
   velocity/gyro, velocity/accel, position/gyro and position/accel. The
   attitude sensitivity is transported through the full increment rotation;
   position inherits the previous velocity sensitivity. These are analytic
   derivatives of the retained step at its constant bias anchor.
3. correctPreintegratedImu moves a stored packet to the estimated bias:
   delta-R becomes delta-R * Exp(J_rotation_gyro * delta-b_gyro), while
   delta-v and delta-p receive their two Jacobian products. rebiasDelta
   performs the same operation on a composed predictor window. This move
   is first order in the bias change; it does not exactly reintegrate a
   finite gyro-bias change. The Kalman measurement updates estimate biases;
   the preintegral Jacobians transport the resulting change into increments.
4. ESKF.predictPreintegrated invokes that correction before predicting
   position, velocity and attitude, and uses the packet's bias Jacobians
   in its covariance transition. The transition is exact at the packet's
   bias anchor for the retained nominal map; moving away from that anchor
   retains the first-order bias-linearization approximation.
5. Both comparison paths exercise this implementation. Retrodiction uses
   generated PreintegrationReplay at 800 Hz and supplies its five blocks
   to the 100 Hz ESKF. The fusion-horizon path generates the same raw-sample
   increments through integrateSample, composes their Jacobians, releases
   anchored packets to the delayed ESKF and reapplies rebiased buffered
   increments to predict the output forward. A raw-IMU path still exists
   when no preintegrated packet is supplied; not every possible ESKF
   configuration therefore uses FOH.
6. Vehicles.Rdd2.WaypointVehicleSystem still defaults useFirstOrderHoldImu
   to false. Its FohGlobalWaypointMission variant explicitly enables FOH.
   The corrected physical bias Jacobians also apply to ZOH, but the FOH
   bracket terms require enabling that option. The benchmark candidate's
   use of FOH must not be described as enabling FOH for every vehicle model.

Fresh verification

Tests.StrapdownPreintegrationJacobianTests and
Tests.StrapdownPreintegrationTests both pass as top-level OpenModelica
simulations from the current worktree. The composed central-difference
regression checks all five physical blocks through three steps, for both
FOH and ZOH, with nonidentity initial rotation, nonzero bias anchor,
changing three-axis inputs, zero rotation and both coefficient branches.
The packed-coordinate tolerance is 2e-8; this is not a universal accuracy
bound. The packet regression checks held-input composition, bias derivatives,
ESKF/UKF packet prediction and FOH improvement against a refined reference.
These constant-parameter tests evaluate assertions during translation and
initialization; their output CSVs contain only time. The normalized log is
acc-preintegration-validation.txt. Local paths are replaced with placeholders;
source and output hashes are in native-noise-manifest.json.

check_paper_series.py also passes twelve independent exact-rational matrix
cases: grades zero through four vanish and grade five has the stated form.
That finite sanity check is not a proof of the general theorem. These checks
are not a fresh full-CI or flight-qualification run.

Limits

The finite-defect flow-error certificate is not evaluated online. The
15-state process-noise covariance uses an equivalent held angular rate and
specific force with a Simpson integral; it does not propagate the exact
sampled FOH packet-noise covariance or its shared-endpoint cross correlation.
The earlier 20,000-draw packet-noise experiment exposes that distinction in
eskf-preintegration-noise.json. Exactness of a mean increment's derivative
must not be described as exact stochastic covariance propagation.

Semi-direct bias geometry is a separate experimental ESKF option, disabled
in the frozen comparison candidate. The paper's FOH bias Jacobians do not
require that option, and they do not make the ESKF a full tangent-group EqF.
The seed-41 stationary attitude/bias overconfidence, native sensor timing
fidelity, full scenario matrix and target resource qualification remain open.
The current evidence supports the preintegration correspondence above, not
completion of the overall estimator-comparison goal.
