Native estimator configuration audit, 7 October 2026
===================================================

The present benchmark does not establish that PX4 EKF2 is generally better than
ArduPilot EKF3. It executes actual pinned native cores, but the replay adapters,
active fusion modes, sensor preprocessing, tuning and information used by the
cores are not equivalent. This also limits the earlier calibrated ESKF win
counts. Those scores remain measurements of their historical configurations;
they must not be presented as an intrinsic algorithm ranking.

The audit ran 24 native replays: two captures, three GPS scenarios, three EKF3
configurations and one diagnostic PX4 configuration per scenario. Captures are
original .6 rad/s seed 7 and held-out .85 rad/s seed 101, high-rate aiding and
nominal delays. All six EKF3 baseline and six instrumented PX4 output CSV hashes
exactly equal the corresponding earlier native-delay outputs. EKF3 baseline
transport counts, payload digests and arrival-trace hashes also match. The
diagnostic instrumentation therefore reproduces those cases, rather than
silently changing the baseline while observing it. This is a two-capture audit,
not a rerun of the full seed/rate/delay matrix.

Both source checkouts remain clean at the pinned commits. No native core was
edited or copied into modelica_models. Existing native binaries/libraries were
reused; only the external PX4 adapter was recompiled with public-getter logging.
The partial Modelica ports are not used in these scores. Evidence files:
  native-configuration-audit-original.json
  native-configuration-audit-held-out.json
  native-configuration-audit-manifest.json

Confirmed adapter error: persistent takeoff expectation
------------------------------------------------------

The external CSV-to-DataFlash adapter sets RFRN TAKEOFF_EXPECTED when arming at
13 s and never clears it. This is a ground-effect hint, not a synonym for being
in flight. EKF3 multiplies barometric observation variance by four while this
flag is set; it can also alter barometric innovation treatment and takeoff
reference updates. The actual Copter vehicle detector clears the expectation
after a sufficient climb or takeoff timeout. The adapter therefore does not
faithfully represent that vehicle-side lifecycle.

The finite_takeoff probe clears only this flag at 18 s, preserving the armed
flag and sensor data. This isolates the persistent-flag effect without using
truth in the estimator. It is a declared five-second phase approximation, not
a reimplementation of Copter's feedback-dependent ground-effect detector. It
improves vertical RMSE in all six audit cases, but slightly worsens horizontal
RMSE. It does not explain away the entire PX4/EKF3 horizontal gap.

Confirmed unequal magnetic fusion modes
---------------------------------------

The capture climbs 2 m. EKF3 is configured with EK3_MAG_CAL=3, the Copter default,
which normally waits for a final in-air yaw/field reset after a 2.5 m climb.
XKFS MAG_FUSION is 1 (heading fusion) in every baseline flight selection record
in all six audited cases. It never becomes 2 (three-axis field fusion).

PX4 is configured with automatic magnetic fusion. Its native policy uses
height above ground, with a 1.5 m magnetic-anomaly threshold. Public getters
show mag_3D in 4572 of 4700 flight updates (97.28%) for every audited case;
mag_heading is set in the other 128. Consequently the existing comparisons
give different tilt/field information to the two native estimators.

finite_takeoff_mag4 additionally sets EK3_MAG_CAL=4, enabling three-axis magnetic
fusion throughout. Its logs confirm MAG_FUSION=2 throughout flight. Results
are mixed; denied yaw worsens substantially on the held-out capture. This is
a sensitivity experiment, not evidence that mode 4 is preferable for flight
or that changing this parameter alone makes the comparison fully equivalent.
The filters also estimate magnetic field states differently from ESKF's frozen
calibrated-field observation.

Confirmed unequal internal rates and timing semantics
----------------------------------------------------

EKF3's pinned sensorIntervalMin_ms is 50 ms. Native ingestion rejects non-IMU
measurements arriving too soon, with sensor-specific strict/non-strict boundary
tests. Providing the same 100/50/50 Hz flow/magnetic/barometric packets does
not give it the same number of updates. Twenty Hz is an upper ingestion bound,
not a measured accepted-update rate; boundary tests and jitter can lower it.

PX4 diagnostic time_last_fuse changes number 4699 for flow and 2349 each for
magnetic/barometric updates across the 4700 sampled flight states. These are
approximately 100/50/50 Hz. They are timestamp-change diagnostics, not a count
of every scalar fusion operation, and may include reset bookkeeping. EKF3's
current logs expose source selection and innovations, but do not independently
count every accepted measurement epoch. Packet delivery equality alone cannot
establish equal internal measurement use.

XKT logs show EKF3 accumulates 12.5 ms delta-angle/delta-velocity packets and
has a settled 12.5 ms average prediction interval (about 80 Hz). PX4's public
getter gives about 10 ms (100 Hz). Native outputs are nevertheless both logged
at 100 Hz and include current output prediction; the earlier scoring compares
those current outputs at common epochs, not delayed states against current
truth. Scoring-grid agreement does not imply equal covariance prediction rates.

The synthetic generator provides instantaneous body velocity/range, converted
to angular flow. EKF3's native interface expects flow averaged over a sampling
interval and subtracts half its fixed 100 ms average flow interval, additional
configured delay and half a filter interval. Its magnetic sample time uses a
fixed 60 ms delay. The synthetic samples do not include corresponding sensor
integration/filtering semantics. PX4 receives explicit original sample times.
The earlier native-delay report disclosed the native timing rules, but that
does not validate the instantaneous-to-averaged measurement mapping. Residual
epoch/preprocessing mismatch remains unresolved. The native cores must not be
patched to remove their production behavior simply to improve these scores.

Other comparison limits
-----------------------

EKF3 uses barometric height; PX4 is configured to also fuse GPS height. Both
receive three-axis GPS velocity. Different vertical source policies mean
different observation information, even with identical delivered packets.

Noise floors and priors remain stack-specific. For example, Copter EKF3's flow
noise setting is .25 rad/s and PX4's high-quality flow floor is .15 rad/s. ESKF's
latest run uses stationary-calibrated IMU white-noise density, while the native
scores reuse their earlier defaults. Numerical parameter equality would not
match physical uncertainty: the process-noise conventions and actual prediction
intervals differ. Bias random walks, magnetic-field states and source policies
also require explicit treatment. No native retuning was done in this audit.

Flow comes from synthetic body velocity and range, not images with texture,
exposure, gyro/image correlations or calibration error. The motion is smooth,
starts from declared rest and has no vibration or magnetic disturbance campaign.
GPS-denied absolute horizontal position is unobservable with these sensors;
startup origin/attitude uncertainty and subsequent drift should be reported
separately. Neither this suite nor the partial ports validate complete firmware,
sensor drivers, lane selection, fault recovery or general flight robustness.

Controlled probe results
------------------------

The sibling JSON files contain every score and diagnostic. The table below is
generated into native-configuration-audit-results.csv. It includes full-flight

Required work before another algorithm-superiority claim
-------------------------------------------------------

1. Correct the vehicle-phase adapter and verify its DAL flags against a native
   Copter trace. Retain old scores as historical; rerun affected configurations.
2. Give the shared sensor generator explicit exposure/averaging/filter-delay
   semantics and verify serialized units, signs, measurement epochs and native
   consumption against production replay/test fixtures. Measure residual delay.
3. Compare supported source/magnetic policies explicitly. Include an experiment
   with a common ingestion rate below EKF3's cap, alongside each stack's normal
   rate. Do not silently disable native rate limits or choose per-case modes.
4. Apply one frozen truth-free calibration to every estimator using its own
   parameter conventions and actual prediction interval. Report default-tuned
   and calibrated stacks separately, including unsupported noise-floor limits.
5. Record actual accepted measurement epochs, rejections, innovations and output
   resets in both native stacks, then rerun the full held-out matrix and native
   replay/SITL cases before drawing broader conclusions. Distinguish available,
   selected and accepted sensor data. Report initialization error separately.

Reproduction
------------

Use the project Nix environment with NumPy, pymavlink and C++. Existing captures
and native builds are read from scratch; new owned work should also use scratch
when writable. Paths below derive from HOME. Choose new work/output filenames;
the probe refuses existing ones and removes only its own successful replay child.

  validation="$HOME/scratch/modelica_models/validation-sources/tools/estimator_comparison/upstream"
  comparison="$HOME/scratch/modelica_models/estimator-comparison"
  work="$HOME/scratch/modelica_models/native-configuration-audit"
  mkdir -p "$work"
  cc -O2 -pipe tools/estimator_comparison/transport_trace.c -lm -o "$work/transport_trace"
  python tools/estimator_comparison/audit_native_configuration.py \
    --harness "$HOME/scratch/estimator-comparison/matched-sensor-replay/harness" \
    --ap-source "$validation/ardupilot" \
    --ap-replay "$comparison/ardupilot/build/sitl/tool/Replay" \
    --px4-source "$validation/px4" \
    --px4-library "$HOME/scratch/modelica_models/readability/native-px4-submodule/libekf2.a" \
    --transport-trace "$work/transport_trace" \
    --capture "$comparison/data/0.6_7" --seed 7 \
    --work "$work/original" --output "$work/original.json"

Repeat with --capture "$HOME/scratch/modelica_models/eskf-improvements/held-out/data/0.85_101"
and --seed 101, selecting new work/output paths. This session used bounded owned
temporary probe directories because scratch and .git are read-only. Source,
binary, adapter, capture and evidence hashes are retained in the manifest.
