RDD2 estimator update audit, 7 October 2026

Vehicles.Rdd2.NavigationEstimator directly extends
Estimation.StrapdownINS.ESKF.Estimator. WaypointVehicleSystem selects that
block as its default replaceable EstimatorModel. There is no copied older
ESKF implementation in the vehicle package.

The current RDD2 deployment export was regenerated with Rumoca 0.10.2 and
compiled with GCC -O2 after all three fixes: valid joint covariance checks,
an explicit covariance-root row snapshot, and assigned raw-prediction
transition. See estimator-theory-manifest.json for source/generated-C hashes.
eskf_aiding.c passed against this export, including simultaneous aiding,
GPS loss/return, independent rejection health and invalid stationary settings.
The current shared correction and raw-prediction kernels passed their
independent reference suites. All 96 updated comparison flight outputs
match the frozen ESKF controls byte for byte.

The comparison driver explicitly enables geometric alignment and vector
magnetic fusion; the RDD2 block's inherited deployment defaults leave both
disabled. Square-root covariance, semi-direct bias and stationary IMU aiding
also remain opt-in. WaypointVehicleSystem defaults to ZOH and has FOH
available through useFirstOrderHoldImu; the comparison driver supplies FOH
packets and all five bias Jacobians.

RDD2's default estimator block uses state retrodiction. The delayed fusion
horizon and forward output predictor are exercised through the separate
comparison composition. The production composed-export limitation remains
documented in docs/delayed-fusion-horizon.md. Fresh RDD2 export and replay
validation must not be described as deployment of that composition or as
whole closed-loop mission qualification.
