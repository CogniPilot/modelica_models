Priors, tuning, source selection and output observers remain stack-specific. This measures configured estimator stacks and does not establish a universal Kalman-filter ranking.

Native state outputs are linearly interpolated to the common scoring epochs; quaternions use sign-continuous normalized interpolation. Flags hold the preceding native value. Missing intervals and extrapolation are refused. Invalid finite states stay in the error metrics. Native source-active flags do not prove individual measurement acceptance.
