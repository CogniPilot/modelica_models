New primitive
LinearAlgebra.covarianceRoot returns lower triangular L from rectangular factor
columns A, with L*L' = A*A' to working precision. Scaled Givens rotations avoid
forming the full covariance during triangularization and do not add noise.
The source uses two clear loops and vector row expressions.
Tests.CovarianceRootTests covers rectangular, rank-deficient and zero inputs.
Tests.CovarianceRootReplay exports dynamic inputs through Rumoca 0.10.2.
