Startup translation diagnosis
=============================

All sixteen GPS-denied reruns reproduced the frozen pressure-study state
CSVs byte for byte. No filter, noise, arrival or scoring setting changed.
startup-diagnosis.json records executable/reference provenance, initial
position/velocity/attitude errors and the exact horizontal MSE decomposition:

  mean(|e(t)|^2) = |e(13)|^2 + mean(|e(t)-e(13)|^2)
                   + 2 e(13) dot mean(e(t)-e(13)).

The hypothesis that the horizontal regression is only a startup translation
is rejected. For seed 7 at 2 m, horizon displacement RMS grows from 0.04592
to 0.06170 m; at 4 m it grows from 0.05084 to 0.07417 m. These are diagnostic
translation-aligned errors, not absolute navigation accuracy and not a new
ranking. Published raw RMS is retained and reconstructed by the decomposition.
Attitude, subsequent velocity and cross coupling still require investigation.
The startup pressure option remains disabled.

Reproduce with tools/estimator_comparison/diagnose_startup_datum.py using
--study frozen-replays.json, --reference the stable native-exposure.json,
--cases the frozen native case directory, --build the four audited pressure
replay executables and fresh owned --work/--output paths under $HOME/scratch.
The tool refuses altered capture, arrival, executable or output hashes.
